# Data sources and acquisition

Raw third-party datasets are not mirrored here unless redistribution and privacy are clearly appropriate. Download from the official source, record retrieval date/version/size/SHA-256, and do not assume a newer download matches the historical analysis input.

| Source | Official site | Public-repository treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil SINAN/DATASUS TB cases | https://datasus.saude.gov.br/acesso-a-informacao/casos-de-tuberculose-desde-2001-sinan/ | Primary surveillance source; raw line-level data excluded. |
| Brazil Ministry of Health SINAN | https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/composicao/svsa/sistemas-de-informacao/sinan/sinan | Official system documentation. |
| IBGE territorial meshes | https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/organizacao-do-territorio/malhas-territoriais/15774-malhas.html | Geographic boundary source for generated maps. |
| IBGE population estimates | https://www.ibge.gov.br/en/statistics/social/population/18448-estimates-of-resident-population-for-municipalities-and-federation-units.html | Population denominator source. |
